# Sources and licenses — Contrails reel

Ledger of every input used by `studio/` to produce *Why do aeroplanes leave white trails?*.
Network policy in the production environment only allowed GitHub, npm and PyPI; NASA,
Wikimedia, Freesound, Poly Haven and HuggingFace were blocked, so **no third-party footage,
photography, music or sound effects are used**. All imagery, music and SFX are generated
procedurally from code in this repository.

## Scientific verification

| Claim on screen / in narration | Basis |
|---|---|
| Contrail = condensation trail; forms when hot, moist exhaust mixes with very cold ambient air (≈ −40 °C or colder at cruise) and the resulting droplets freeze into ice crystals | Schmidt–Appleman criterion — Schumann, U. (1996) *On conditions for contrail formation from aircraft exhausts*, Meteorol. Z. 5, 4–23; Kärcher, B. (2018) *Formation and radiative forcing of contrail cirrus*, Nature Communications 9, 1824 |
| Water vapour is a combustion product of jet fuel (≈1.23 kg H₂O per kg kerosene) | Lee, D.S. et al. (2021) *The contribution of global aviation to anthropogenic climate forcing for 2000 to 2018*, Atmospheric Environment 244, 117834 |
| Droplets form on exhaust soot/sulfur particles and ambient aerosol, then freeze; crystals are micrometre-sized | Kärcher (2018); Schumann (1996) |
| Persistence controlled by ambient humidity w.r.t. ice: sub-saturated (RHi < 100 %) → short-lived; ice-supersaturated (RHi > 100 %) → persists and spreads (contrail cirrus) | Kärcher (2018); Schumann (1996); NOAA/NWS JetStream "Contrails" educational page |
| Cruise altitude ≈ 10–12 km; ambient ≈ −40 to −60 °C | International Standard Atmosphere (ISO 2533:1975): −56.5 °C above 11 km |
| Visible trail starts a short distance behind the engine (mixing/cooling time) | Schumann (1996); Kärcher (2018) |
| CTA question — do contrails affect climate? (answer, not revealed on screen: **yes**; contrail cirrus is a net warming effective radiative forcing, ≈57 mW m⁻² (17–98) for 2018, comparable to/greater than aviation CO₂ ERF, with large uncertainty) | Lee et al. (2021); IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch. 7 (2021) |

Primary-source URLs could not be fetched from this environment (blocked hosts); the citations
above are standard peer-reviewed references and should be spot-checked by a human before publishing.
