# Aircraft Contrails — Roman Hinglish reel

**Status:** Editorial working draft; confirm narration and CTA before rendering.
**Target:** 40–45 seconds, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920.

## Hook (0–5s)
"Kabhi aasman mein aeroplane ke peeche ye lambi safed line dekhi hai? Kya plane aasman mein dhuaan chhod raha hai? Ya phir kuch aur?"

## Curiosity (5–13s)
"Interesting baat ye hai ki ye line har aeroplane ke peeche nahi dikhti! Kabhi ye kuch seconds mein gayab ho jaati hai… aur kabhi ghanton tak aasman mein bani rehti hai!"

## Science reveal (13–27s)
"Actually, ise kehte hain Contrail — yaani Condensation Trail! Jab jet engine se nikli garam, water-vapour wali exhaust bahut thandi hawa se milti hai, toh tiny ice crystals ban jaate hain. Aur wahi humein aasman mein safed line ki tarah dikhte hain!"

## Atmospheric twist (27–35s)
"Lekin twist ye hai — contrail kitni der rahegi, ye mainly depend karta hai upper atmosphere ki humidity par! Dry air mein jaldi gayab, aur humid air mein kaafi der tak reh sakti hai!"

## CTA (35–45s)
"Ab ek tricky sawaal! Agar ek jet engine band ho jaaye, toh kya uske peeche white trail phir bhi ban sakti hai?

A — Bilkul nahi
B — Haan, kuch conditions mein
C — Sirf rainy season mein

Comment A, B ya C! Follow Defence Pathshala — kyunki yahan Science ratte nahi, Decode karte hain!"

**Answer:** B. Aerodynamic condensation trails are possible under suitable conditions even without engine exhaust. Ensure the visual distinguishes aerodynamic condensation from exhaust contrails.

**Accuracy:** Exhaust contrails form when moist engine exhaust mixes with sufficiently cold ambient air and the resulting droplets freeze; atmospheric ice supersaturation controls persistence. Air temperature, humidity, pressure, and engine characteristics matter.
